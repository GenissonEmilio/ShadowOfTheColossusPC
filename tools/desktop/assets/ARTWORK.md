# Launcher artwork

`hero.png` and `icon.png` are adaptations of the illustration supplied by the
project owner on 29 October 2026. They were edited with the built-in imagegen
tool. They are separate from the GPL licensing of the launcher source code;
no license to Sony / Team Ico characters or trademarks is granted by this repo.
The original attachment is preserved in the owner's Downloads folder.

The banner has a 3:1 composition and no baked-in UI text. GTK draws the real
labels and buttons above it. The icon has an actual alpha channel, and is used
by the sidebar, window and desktop shortcut. Artwork is loaded from bundled
assets and does not require a network connection.

## Banner prompt

Use case: precise-object-edit. Asset type: wide desktop game launcher hero banner, 3:1 landscape composition. Edit target: supplied illustration of Wander raising his sword on the giant colossus with cyan eyes. Preserve the original illustrated appearance, the character pose, sword, the colossus face, cyan glowing eyes, and birds. Remove the entire purple console frame and PS2 badge, without replacing them with any text or marks. Recompose for a wide 3:1 landscape banner: the full sword, Wander and both eyes must fit comfortably in the RIGHT half of the image; the LEFT half is quiet, almost solid deep forest green #1f2b24 to hold actual UI text later. Blend the illuminated misty sky into that dark green smoothly across the middle, with no vertical seam. Match an atmospheric dark green, muted gold and cyan game launcher. No lettering, no UI, no frame, no icons, no logos. Keep the sword tip and both glowing eyes inside the canvas, with some breathing room. Finished production artwork, not a screenshot.

## Icon prompt

Use case: background-extraction. Asset type: transparent square desktop launcher icon and small sidebar emblem. Edit the supplied illustration: retain the colossus head with both glowing cyan eyes, dark shaggy fur, its forehead crest, and the full silhouette of Wander standing on top raising his sword. Preserve the original style, colors and pose. Remove the purple border, the PS2 badge, all text, sky and birds entirely. Real transparent background outside the silhouette. Compose the head centered, approximately symmetrical, sword tip inside the square canvas with 5% clear padding. Fur and head occupy the lower two-thirds; Wander and raised sword occupy the upper third. At the bottom, fade the black neck smoothly into transparency. High contrast cyan eyes readable at small icon sizes. No frame, no console logo, no text, no extra objects.
